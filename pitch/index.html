01 — What is SpotMo

A live map of what's actually happening nearby.

SpotMo is a location-based event discovery platform — open it, and see gigs, exhibits, markets and gatherings plotted on a map in real time, the moment they're posted.

Built for local communities — not for major entertainment companies.

02 — The Problem

Most discovery platforms only show you what already has a marketing budget.

Everything else — the events people would actually love to find — stays invisible.

01

Small events struggle with visibility

A great gig with 40 people deserves to be found as easily as one with 4,000.

02

Artists rely on word of mouth alone

Independent artists are often left with only social media and whoever happens to share their post.

03

Great nights never reach new crowds

Venues host amazing, recurring events that only their existing regulars ever hear about.

04

Platforms favor the already-famous

Existing event platforms usually prioritize ticketed, sponsored, or high-profile listings first.

03 — Our Mission

Our mission is to give every event a chance to be discovered.

Whether it's —

A local band A coffee shop acoustic night A student art exhibit A charity event A neighborhood market A cultural gathering

Every one of them deserves an audience.

Fast event publishing
